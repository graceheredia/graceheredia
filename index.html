ABOUT ME

I was born and raised in Fort Wayne Indiana and have been interested in healthcare since I was ten. I am majoring in pre-nursing because I love science and combining it with caring for people is the best profession for me. Although I do not play soccer, I enjoy watching it and my favorite team is Real Madrid. I supported Croatia in the World Cup due to Luka Modric being my favorite player. I enjoy going thrifting for clothes and shopping whenever I have the money. I also enjoy going outside to take walks and exploring new places. My favorite activity is finding small businesses or cafes and meeting the people who run them. In high school I was a social media manager for my friends club called Culturwise and a co-president of another cultural club named cultures Connect. I enjoy meeting people from many different types of Cultures and learning how diverse the world is, it allows me to appreciate it more. I am planning on joining clubs for students In health care or nursing as well as latino clubs. I think reading is a very interesting hobby to have. Even though I do not really read in my free time, whenever I do I always learn new information and become surprised on how much I actually enjoy it. Reading can be fun depending on what the content is. I do not usually choose to do it because I am busy with other things but if I am assigned something, or just find something interesting to read it is always a good experience. On the other hand I do not really enjoy writing because I do not view myself as a very creative person. I believe writing takes a lot of skill and thought that I do not utilize enough. I understand how important writing is though because it allows myself to express my thoughts.

Project 1 Pre-writing Tree

View Project 1 Pre-writing Tree

My phenomenon is tension between parents and ADHD medication. Other potential phenomena that I could have chosen include How people conform to their environments, internal hyperactivity within inattentive ADHD, and younger generations facing pressure about being medicated for ADHD. I chose the tension with parents specifically because I face this problem every day, and I highly relate to it. Conformity was a broad phenomenon that I noticed in my inquiries, but when I looked deeply into them, I saw a specific pattern. My thoughts were constantly changing, and I was unable to make consistent observations for me to even see a pattern most likely because of my ADHD. This led me to internal hyperactivity within inattentive ADHD. I still felt like it wasn’t specific enough thought and it would be hard for me to find sources about it. I eventually came down to tension between parents and ADHD medication, mostly due to my lived experiences rather than my observations. My observations allowed me to see how my brain attempted to make clear and deep thoughts, but they weren’t thorough enough. Seeing my disorder in my writing made me recognize that I could choose a phenomenon based on what I experience living with. I narrowed down my options to medication because it relates to what we have been going over in class. I saw a pattern of stigma with my medication and ultimately related it to what I personally struggle with. Which is trying to relieve the tension between being medicated and my parents' opposition to it. I interpret my phenomenon to be an annoying struggle, but it’s normal for me. I never thought too deep into it because I assumed that most people’s parents wouldn’t want them on medication either. That made me wonder, how do other people's parents feel about it? What kind of struggles do they face? Are they subtle or do they interfere with life dramatically? For me, the tension is subtle, that is why I never noticed it. Some cultural artifacts that relate to my phenomenon include a Netflix documentary: Take Your Pills, South Parks Episode “Timmy 2000”, and the CIBA 1970 Ritalin Advertisement. I believe one of the strongest cultural artifacts is Take Your Pills documentary. This is because it heavily creates a stigma toward ADHD medications, showing the “abusive” side of it. I actually watched this documentary with my mom, and it came with a lot of tension between us. The documentary shows a lot more of the negative side of the medication, and very little of the positive. When watching this with her she continuously made negative comments towards me because I was medicated, trying to make me change my mind about it. Because the documentary focuses more on how people abuse ADHD medications to enhance their performance, it undermines the actual medical use for them. It can expand my current interpretation into parents who may believe that ADHD medications are abused so much to the point that they may fear putting their child onto it. It can complicate my interpretation by seeing it as they feel like a bad parent putting their child onto this medication due to the drug being marketed as not a medical necessity, but to be able to have their child perform as well as others in school. While this could be about parents not seeing the actual medical necessity for the medication, it could also be about their fears of having their child depend on a drug to function for the rest of their life, as well as such a strong stigma around the medication making them feel like a bad parent for complying with it.
